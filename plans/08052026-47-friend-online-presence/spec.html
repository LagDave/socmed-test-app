Socmed Plan Spec · Seq 47

Friend-only online presence

Status: In Progress

Plan folder: plans/08052026-47-friend-online-presence · Execution branch: kylie/chat-online-status → dev

Why

Mutual friends need a timely online/last-active indication without exposing presence to non-friends. The feature must remain correct when a socket disconnects, reconnects, or its database timestamp write fails.

What and Done

  • Expose presence only to mutual friends over HTTP and realtime.
  • Use one friend-presence realtime channel for profile, inbox, and open-thread consumers.
  • Persist last-active when possible but always emit the offline transition.
  • Ship only the presence migration, with an idempotent conversations index.
  • Record browser/database acceptance before finalization.

Context and constraints

Relevant files: src/realtime/PresenceRealtime.ts, src/services/ProfileService.ts, frontend/src/contexts/FriendPresenceProvider.tsx, frontend/src/components/ConversationListRow.tsx, frontend/src/pages/MessagesPage.tsx, and database/migrations/20260805120000_user_last_active.ts. The Maps are deliberately process-local for the current single-node runtime; a multi-instance deployment requires shared presence state. Preserve friend-only authorization and no foreign migrations.

Risk

Level 2 — Concern. Presence is realtime state. A failed last-active write must not leave friends incorrectly online; migrations must be re-runnable. Mitigate through offline emission after logged persistence failure, idempotent DDL, and staged multi-user acceptance.
