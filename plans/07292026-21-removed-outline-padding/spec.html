Why

Friends, Profile, Notifications, and Messages wrap their content in a nested shell: an outer soft-page-canvas plate with px-4 py-6 sm:px-6, then an inner bordered card. That outer padding reads as a dead “outline” gutter between the soft plate and the card. It adds visual noise without aiding hierarchy.

BEFORE                          AFTER
┌─ soft-page-canvas ──────┐     ┌─ soft-page-canvas ──────┐
│  pad                     │     │┌─ card (flush) ────────┐│
│  ┌─ card ─────────────┐  │     ││ content               ││

What

Strip the outer padding classes from every in-scope soft-page-canvas wrapper so the inner card(s) sit flush against the soft plate edges. Keep the soft canvas background, -mx-4 bleed, and rounded plate. Do not remove the soft-page-canvas pattern itself.

In scope pages: Friends · Profile · Notifications · Messages (inbox + thread) · Account Settings (same pattern; included for consistency).

Done when:

  • No visible padded gutter between soft-page-canvas and inner card on those pages
  • Soft plate + inner card borders/radius still present
  • Feed and other non-canvas pages unchanged
  • Frontend typecheck/build pass; acceptance checklist passes or waived in writing
