Profile-update shadow and feed-card spacing

Plan folderplans/08072026-profile-feed-spacing
Execution basedev at c77fb30
SizeSmall — two scoped visual adjustments, no new dependencies or API work
Targetdev

Why

The profile-picture activity card’s image has both a blurred backdrop and a broad 10px/28px drop shadow. Together they produce the visible gray halo in the supplied screenshot. Separately, the home-feed cards use a 20px list gap, making successive cards feel more disconnected than intended.

What

Apply exactly two visual changes:

  1. Keep the profile-picture image dimensions and ring unchanged, remove the diffuse decorative halo, and replace its broad shadow with one tighter, lower-opacity shadow.
  2. Reduce the raw vertical separation between consecutive home-feed cards and feed loading skeletons from 20px (space-y-5) to 4px (space-y-1), letting the existing card elevation shadows provide the remaining visual separation.

Done when: profile-picture activity images retain a subtle lift without a cloudy perimeter; normal cards and skeletons use a consistent 4px layout rhythm, with card shadows as the remaining visual separation; post actions, media sizing, post content, and all feed behavior remain unchanged.
