Why

Plans 17–23 shipped a working 1:1 messenger with realtime delivery and a friend picker, but the Messages surface still reads utilitarian next to Notifications and the in-flight Feed (Plan 36) and Friends (Plan 37) upgrades. The inbox stacks three separate feed-card shells; errors sit outside the card; conversation rows lack the unread dot and row density of NotificationRow. The thread view works but the composer and bubbles feel like bare form controls rather than a cohesive chat product.

This is a visual polish pass on /messages and /messages/:conversationId. No new features, no API or socket behavior changes.
